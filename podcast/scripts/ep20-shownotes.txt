Ep20: The Six Doors of the Night

Three people cannot sleep tonight — but their nights break in three completely different ways. One mind will not switch off. One body jolts awake at two in the morning. One person sleeps eight hours and still wakes feeling flattened.

This episode uses the classical Chinese medicine image of six doors, plus two familiar side rooms, to build a practical field guide to sleepless nights. The point is not to diagnose yourself from the clock. It is to notice the hour, the sensation that wakes you, and how the morning feels — then bring a clearer description into a real medical conversation when sleep keeps disrupting your days.

We look at the restless mind, the one-to-three alarm, the pre-dawn waking, the frightened watchman, the person who sleeps but wakes wrecked, and the stomach still digesting dinner. Then we place the old map beside modern sleep care, including CBT-I and the conditions that require clinical assessment.
