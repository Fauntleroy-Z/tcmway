One traveler at a station door wants a scarf back. Another barely feels the draft. What would an early reader of Chinese medicine notice about their collars, their skin, and the timing of the chill? Ollie reads two Taiyang passages from the Discussion of Cold Damage and follows one small difference between them: whether sweat had already appeared. Along the way, he explains what Wei Qi meant to those readers and where that vocabulary reaches its limits.
