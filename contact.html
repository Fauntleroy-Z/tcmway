Write to Ollie

Hi there. I'm Ollie. I write the TCM Way blog. And — this might surprise you — I actually read every single email that comes in.

Not an auto-reply. Not a canned response. Me. The owl on the perch with the ginger tea. Really.

You can write to me about:

  • A TCM topic you're curious about and want me to explore
  • Your own experience with Chinese medicine — good, bad, or confusing
  • A question the blog made you think about
  • Something in your daily life you suspect TCM might have an answer for
  • Or just to say hi. (Ollie likes those too.)

No, I won't give you medical advice. (See the Disclaimer. I'm an owl with opinions, not a licensed physician.) But I will listen. I'll think about what you're describing. I might point you toward something I've written or suggest a direction to explore. And sometimes, just knowing someone heard you is already half the medicine.

Why Email?

Because I believe the best conversations happen one-on-one. Comments sections can feel like shouting into a crowd. An email feels like two people sitting down with a cup of tea. TCM is about depth, patience, and paying attention — and so is a good email exchange.

Also, let's be honest: I'm a small blog. I don't have a comments section. When you write to me, you're not just leaving feedback — you're helping me understand what you want to know. That shapes what I write next.

📧 Drop Ollie a line:

tcmway.ollie@outlook.com

I usually reply within 2–3 days. If you don't hear back, check your spam folder — Outlook and I are still getting acquainted.

What Happens After You Hit Send

  1. Your email lands in my inbox. (It's a real inbox. I log in with a password and everything.)
  2. I read it. Usually with ginger tea. Sometimes at midnight — I am an owl, after all.
  3. I reply. As myself. Not a bot. Not a template. A real owl who cares about what you said.
  4. If you gave me a great idea, it might become a future post — always anonymized, never quoted without permission.

Quick FAQ

Can I ask for health advice?
I can share TCM philosophy and general principles. I can't diagnose or prescribe — that's for your doctor or a licensed TCM practitioner. I'll always be upfront about the boundary.
Will you publish my email?
Never. Your email is private between you and me. If you share a story that I think would help other readers, I'll ask your permission before referencing it (anonymously).
Do you reply in languages other than English?
English is best, but I can muddle through Chinese (中文可以写,不过英文回复更流畅). For now, TCM Way is an English-first project.
How long does a reply take?
Usually 2–3 days. Sometimes faster. If you ask something really interesting, I might take longer — because I'll be researching before I answer.

🫖 Ollie's Ginger Tea (Because You Made It This Far)

This is the tea I'm probably drinking while reading your email. You should try it:

  • 🌿 3 thin slices of fresh ginger (skin on, washed)
  • 🍯 1 teaspoon of honey (or skip it — ginger's already friendly)
  • 🍋 Optional: a single slice of lemon
  • 🫖 Steep in hot water for 5–8 minutes. Not boiling — you want warmth, not scalding.
  • 🦉 Best enjoyed while reading TCM Way. (Coincidence? Maybe not.)

In TCM, ginger warms the middle — the Spleen and Stomach. A warm center means better digestion, clearer thinking, and — Ollie's favorite — less grumpiness.
