Subject: You don't need more willpower. You need warmer tea.

Hey friend,

Most people treat sleep like a boxing match.

They climb into bed and square up. *I will sleep. I will stay asleep. If I wake up at 3 AM, I will wrestle my brain back into submission.*

I get it. I've done it too. (Owls are nocturnal, so my version involves lying in a tree at 10 AM trying to force myself into daytime consciousness while a woodpecker hammers a beat three branches over. It doesn't work either.)

But here's what Chinese medicine taught me, and it's the single most useful thing I can share with you:

**Sleep is not a battle of willpower. It's a rhythm waiting to be restored.**

In TCM, falling asleep and staying asleep isn't about effort. It's about whether your internal environment is warm enough, calm enough, and timed well enough for your body to do what it already knows how to do.

Think of a campfire. You don't *force* a campfire to burn. You give it dry wood, arrange it carefully, and let the flame catch. Sleep works the same way. The problem is, most of us spend our evenings doing the equivalent of throwing wet logs on the fire and yelling "BURN, PLEASE."

Cold drinks at dinner. Racing thoughts. Screens blasting until the moment we close our eyes. Then we lie there wondering why the fire won't catch.

**So what's the five-minute fix?**

Warm ginger tea after dinner.

That's it. Not a supplement stack. Not a $4,000 mattress. Not another meditation app with a free trial you'll forget to cancel.

Three thin slices of fresh ginger in hot water, steeped ten minutes, sipped slowly about half an hour after your evening meal. It's Day 1 of your guide — the simplest thing on the list, and the one that surprises people most.

Why it works: ginger warms what TCM calls the Spleen and Stomach — your digestive fire. When that fire is strong, your body processes dinner and settles into rest mode instead of running a night shift it never signed up for. When it's weak (thanks to cold food, stress, irregular eating), you go to bed with a backlog of work your system never finished. A warm digestive system means a quiet nervous system. A quiet nervous system means sleep that actually restores you.
