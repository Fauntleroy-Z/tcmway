🏯 5 Ancient Summer Hacks That Still Work

How people survived 40°C summers without air conditioning. Five methods — tested for two thousand years.

1. Morning Watermelon (with a pinch of salt)

Watermelon clears Summer Heat — the TCM term for what builds up after days of high temperatures. A pinch of salt prevents the cooling from going too far and shocking the Spleen.

2. The Afternoon Nap with a Bamboo Mat

Summer belongs to the Heart — the Heart needs midday rest. 20 minutes, flat on a cool surface. Not lazy. Strategic.

3. Mung Bean Soup, Not Iced Drinks

Ice constricts. Mung beans cool gently from the inside — the same principle your car radiator uses. Cook until the beans split, drink the liquid, leave the beans.

4. Feet in Cool Water Before Bed

Heat rises in the evening. Cool water on the soles signals the entire body to release excess heat through the Kidney channel. 5 minutes before sleep.

5. Open Opposite Windows

Ancient Chinese houses were designed for cross-ventilation. Two windows on opposite walls, both open. Even in a modern apartment — this creates a wind tunnel that drops room temperature 2-3°C.
