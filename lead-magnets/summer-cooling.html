🧊 7 Days of Summer Cooling

Ancient Chinese wisdom for the hottest days — what to eat, drink, and do.

Day 1: Swap the Smoothie for Congee

Cold breakfasts shock the digestive fire. Start your morning with warm millet congee — it cools from the inside out.

Day 3: 15 Minutes of Green Time

Summer belongs to Fire — too much Fire and the Heart overheats. Sit under a tree, no phone. Let the Wood balance the Fire.

Day 5: A Cup of Chrysanthemum

The sage of summer teas. Clears heat from the eyes, calms the spirit, gently lowers internal temperature.

Day 7: One Cold Thing. Just One.

Your body has been cooling for a week. Now it can handle it. Ice cream, watermelon, a cold beer — pick one. Enjoy it without guilt.
