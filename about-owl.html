Why an Owl?

In cultures across the world, the owl symbolizes wisdom — a creature that sees in the dark where others are blind. Traditional Chinese Medicine is exactly that: a way of seeing patterns where others see chaos. A screech owl, with its distinctive ear tufts and piercing golden eyes, doesn't need a floodlight. It just needs to pay attention.

Plus, owls are cute. And if we're going to talk about your liver Qi stagnation, we might as well have a cute owl do it — wearing a warm brown pendant his grandmother gave him, sipping ginger tea, and occasionally nodding off mid-paragraph.

"The body doesn't speak English or Chinese. It speaks patterns. Ollie's here to translate."
