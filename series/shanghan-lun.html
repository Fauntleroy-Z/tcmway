🛡️ The Six Stages: A Reading Path

Zhang Zhongjing's Shanghan Lun (Treatise on Cold Damage, 200 AD) maps illness as a journey through six defensive layers — from the body's surface to its deepest core. This 10-article series follows that path, one stage at a time. Start at the top and work your way down.

1
Taiyang 太阳 · Greater Yang
The body's outermost shield — where every invasion begins.
  • Wind-Cold Is Not A Weather Report The first question: are you sweating? Gui Zhi Tang or Ma Huang Tang?
↓
2
Shaoyang 少阳 · Lesser Yang
Caught between surface and interior — the pivot point.
  • When Your Body Is Stuck Between Two Fires Shivering and burning up — the body's most confusing immune response.
↓
3
Yangming 阳明 · Yang Brightness
Full-blown internal heat — the fire that won't go out.
  • Your Fever Might Be Your Friend What if a fever isn't failure but your body fighting at full capacity?
  • When the Fire Won't Go Out — Understanding the Yangming Stage High fever, drenching sweats, unquenchable thirst — Bai Hu Tang's domain.
↓
4
Taiyin 太阴 · Greater Yin
When cold reaches the interior — digestion fails.
  • When Food Stops Being Food — Understanding the Taiyin Stage Bloating, exhaustion after eating — when your digestive engine needs rekindling.
↓
5
Shaoyin 少阴 · Lesser Yin
The deepest cold — when the body's vital fire flickers.
  • The Most Dangerous Quiet — Why "I Just Want to Sleep" Is Not Laziness When disease reaches your body's deepest level and sleep doesn't restore you.
  • Stop Fighting — Start Warming: Why "Yang Tonics" Are Not About Heat Warming herbs restart your inner fire — chronic cold isn't a personality quirk.
  • The Fire Inside You Is Going Out (And You Don't Even Know It) Your Life Gate Fire — and how modern life is slowly blowing it out.
↓
6
Jueyin 厥阴 · Terminal Yin
The final layer — where hot and cold collide.
  • When the Body Reaches Its Last Line Hot above, cold below — your body feels like civil war. The Jueyin paradox.
  • How a Cold Becomes Cancer (Over Years) A cold suppressed today becomes cancer decades later — and how to stop it.

The Six Stages aren't just medical theory. They're a map of how life itself moves through us — from surface to depth, from health to illness, and back again. Zhang Zhongjing didn't write a textbook. He wrote a survival guide for the human body, and it's still the best one we have.

— Ollie
