# N1 Comic Script: Yin & Yang ≠ Good vs Evil
## 4-Panel Comic — Seesaw Metaphor

**Panel 1 (Balanced)**
Scene: Sunny park, wooden seesaw. Ollie stands beside it, one wing pointing at the seesaw.
Left seat: crescent moon + label "YIN" (cool blue-white tone)
Right seat: sun + label "YANG" (warm golden tone)
Seesaw: perfectly horizontal, balanced.
Ollie speech bubble: "Meet the oldest seesaw in medical history."

**Panel 2 (Yang Up)**
Scene: Same park, same seesaw.
Ollie has hopped onto the YANG (sun/right) side, pushing it DOWN.
YIN (moon/left) side goes UP.
Ollie looks up thoughtfully.
Caption below: "Yang rises, Yin falls." 
Lighting: warm golden glow on right, subtle on left.

**Panel 3 (Yin Up)**
Scene: Same park, same seesaw. 
Ollie has moved to the YIN (moon/left) side, pushing it DOWN.
YANG (sun/right) side goes UP.
Ollie looks calm and steady.
Caption below: "Yin rises, Yang falls."
Lighting: cool blue-white glow on left, subtle on right.

**Panel 4 (Dynamic Balance)**
Scene: Same park, late afternoon/dusk transition.
Ollie stands BESIDE the seesaw (not on it), gesturing toward it with one wing.
The seesaw is in gentle motion — subtle back-and-forth, neither side fully up or down.
A soft wave/rhythm line flows across the scene.
Ollie speech bubble: "Neither side is winning. That is the point."
Ollie expression: warm, knowing smile.
