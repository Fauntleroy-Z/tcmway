# Comic Script — Article #12: The Liver Rules Everything

## Panel 1
**Scene**: Ollie standing outside a medical building with five separate clinic signs: ORTHOPEDICS, GASTROENTEROLOGY, PSYCHIATRY, NEUROLOGY, SLEEP CLINIC. Ollie holds a long list of symptoms in one wing, looking bewildered and slightly overwhelmed.
**Ollie expression**: Confused, head tilted. One wing scratches his head.
**Caption**: "Shoulder pain. IBS. Headaches. Anxiety. Insomnia. Five specialists. Five waiting rooms. Zero answers."

## Panel 2
**Scene**: A tree with deep, visible roots. One main root is labeled "LIVER." From this root, branches sprout with labels: HEADACHE, SHOULDER PAIN, IBS, ANXIETY, INSOMNIA, ACID REFLUX. Ollie stands beside the tree, pointing at the Liver root with one wing, looking at the reader with a knowing expression.
**Ollie expression**: Eyebrow raised, gentle "aha" look.
**Caption**: "What if they all grew from the same root?"

## Panel 3
**Scene**: A winding river landscape. Upstream, a large beaver dam blocks the river. Downstream, five small towns along the riverbank are suffering different problems — one is flooding, one is dried up, one has dead fish, one has no boats, one is on fire. Ollie stands on a bridge in the middle, observing calmly.
**Ollie expression**: Thoughtful, one wing on chin.
**Caption**: "In TCM, your Liver is the river. One blockage upstream. Chaos everywhere downstream."

## Panel 4
**Scene**: Ollie walks confidently toward a single clinic with a warm wooden sign that reads "TCM." Behind him, the five hospital wings from Panel 1 are closing their doors, shutters coming down. Ollie looks back with a small, relieved smile. A green sprout grows from the path ahead of him.
**Ollie expression**: Calm, relieved, walking with purpose.
**Caption**: "One organ. One pattern. Ten symptoms. Infinite relief."

## Visual Notes
- Style: 2D flat hand-drawn, warm earthy tones
- Ollie: 朱砂红 head (#b83a2a), 杏色 belly (#e8a87c), gold yin-yang pendant
- Background: Warm cream (#fdf6ee) palette
- No cute/manga style — keep it wise, warm, slightly whimsical
- Tree in Panel 2 should feel ancient and wise, like an old oak
- River scene in Panel 3 should have varied landscape — hills, forests, small towns
