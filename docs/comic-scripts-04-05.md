# Four-Panel Comic Scripts for TCM Way Blog

> **Author:** Ollie (TCM Owl)
> **Style:** 2D flat hand-drawn, warm earth tones (caramel brown + sage green + cream white), no 3D rendering
> **Character:** Ollie — warm brown owl with emerald green eyes, yin-yang necklace, sitting on a mortar (药臼)
> **Voice:** Curious, non-judgmental, simplifies complex concepts, occasionally dozes off, ginger tea enthusiast
> **Format:** 4 panels per comic, horizontal layout (2x2 or 1x4)

## Comic #04 — "Why Your Body Is Like a Traffic Jam"

**Article:** `04-traffic-jam-inside-your-body.html`
**Core Concept:** Qi Stagnation = traffic jam inside your body
**Target Image File:** `../images/comic-04-traffic-jam.webp`
**Recommended Layout:** 1x4 horizontal strip or 2x2 grid

---

### Panel 1: The Setup — Everything's There But Nothing Moves

**Visual Description:**
Ollie is sitting at a modern office desk, looking at a computer screen. He's wearing a tiny necktie (suggesting his "day job" in international trade). The desk has a cup of cold tea, a stack of papers, and a potted plant that looks slightly wilted. Ollie's expression is one of vague unease — one wing is resting on the desk, his eyes are half-lidded, and there's a small thought bubble above his head showing a confused "???" symbol. His shoulders are visibly hunched up near his ears.

**Background Details:**
- A window showing gray cityscape (suggesting stress/urban environment)
- A clock on the wall showing it's only 10 AM but he already feels drained
- Subtle visual metaphor: behind Ollie, there's a faint ghost-image of tiny cars on a highway inside his body outline (very subtle, like a watermark)

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "You slept fine. You ate breakfast. Your to-do list is manageable. And yet... something just feels *off*. Like your body is wearing clothes two sizes too small."

**Mood:** Relatable fatigue, the universal "I don't know what's wrong but something's wrong" feeling.

---

### Panel 2: The Metaphor — Accident on the Highway

**Visual Description:**
The scene shifts to a metaphorical view INSIDE a human body silhouette (simplified outline, cream-colored). Inside this body-highway, we see:
- Tiny glowing orbs representing Qi energy (warm amber color)
- These orb-cars are supposed to be flowing smoothly along meridian-rivers/highways
- But there's been an ACCIDENT — represented by a big red "X" mark or a frowning emoji-icon labeled "Unprocessed Emotion" blocking the main highway
- Behind the blockage, the Qi-orbs are piling up in a massive traffic jam
- Some frustrated Qi-orbs are honking (tiny "beep beep!" text)
- One Qi-orb is taking an angry detour and causing side-effects (shown as a small headache icon appearing near where it goes off-road)

Ollie is standing to the right of this body-silhouette, wearing a construction vest and holding a traffic cone, looking at the mess with a knowing/slightly exasperated expression. His yin-yang necklace is glowing faintly.

**Background Details:**
- The meridian highways should look somewhat like actual roadways but with a TCM aesthetic (gentle curves, not rigid grids)
- The accident site could have little signs like "Frustration Pile-Up" or "Deadline Crash Zone"
- Color scheme: warm amber flowing Qi → stuck red/orange jam area → clear green downstream areas

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "In TCM, this is called *Qi stagnation*. Something blocked the flow. The backup created secondary problems. And your body started sending signals — first in whispers, then in shouts."

**Mood:** Educational "aha!" moment — the visualization makes the abstract concept click.

---

### Panel 3: The Cause — It's Not Just One Thing

**Visual Description:**
Split into 3-4 mini-scenes showing common stagnation triggers, arranged around Ollie who is now sitting cross-legged on his mortar, holding a mug of ginger tea, looking thoughtful:

1. **Top-left mini-scene:** A stick-figure (or simplified bird-figure) sitting at a desk for TOO LONG — legs are stiff, butt has fused to chair (exaggerated comedy). Label: "Prolonged Sitting"
2. **Top-right mini-scene:** A figure holding in anger — face turning red, a thought bubble shows suppressed emotions as a ball being shoved down. Label: "Unprocessed Emotions"
3. **Bottom-left mini-scene:** A figure eating a giant greasy burger while stressed (sweat drop on forehead). Label: "Diet Under Stress"
4. **Bottom-right mini-scene:** A couch-potato figure with remote control. Label: "Lack of Movement"

Ollie is pointing to each scene with different wings, as if presenting a case.

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "The most common culprit? Emotions that didn't get processed. Unprocessed energy has to go somewhere — and if it doesn't go out... it goes IN."

**Mood:** Informative but lighthearted — recognizing yourself in these scenarios.

---

### Panel 4: The Fix — Be the Flowing Water

**Visual Description:**
Ollie is now HAPPY and ACTIVE — doing a joyful stretch/yoga pose on top of his mortar (one wing up, one foot lifted, looking energized). Around him, the previously-stuck Qi-orbs are now flowing freely in beautiful smooth curves through a clean meridian-highway. The background is brighter, sunnier (maybe sunrise colors).

Below or beside Ollie, show 3 simple icons representing the key tips:
1. 🚶‍♂️ Walking/movement icon (labeled "Move")
2. 😮‍💨 Sighing/exhale icon (labeled "Let it Out")  
3. 🍵 Warm soup/tea icon (labeled "Warm & Cooked")

The final panel should feel hopeful and actionable — not preachy, but encouraging.

**Ollie's Signature Line:**
> **Ollie:** "The Chinese have a saying: *Flowing water does not stagnation.* Be the water. Move. Sigh. Eat warm food. And check your shoulders — they're probably still by your ears."

**Small signature tag at bottom-right corner:**
> *(Ollie drops his traffic cone. It makes a satisfying clunk sound.)*

**Mood:** Hopeful, empowering, gentle humor. Reader should feel "I can actually DO this."

## Comic #05 — "Your Body Runs on a Clock You Didn't Set"

**Article:** `05-body-clock-you-didnt-set.html`
**Core Concept:** Ziwu Liuzhu — TCM Body Clock with 12 two-hour organ windows
**Target Image File:** `../images/comic-05-body-clock.webp`
**Recommended Layout:** 2x2 grid (allows more space for the clock visual)

---

### Panel 1: The Pattern — "3 AM Again?!"

**Visual Description:**
A bedroom scene at night (dark blue/purple tones, moon visible through window). Ollie is sitting up in bed, feathers disheveled, wide awake, staring at an alarm clock that clearly shows **2:47 AM**. His eyes are big and tired, with dark circles under them (exaggerated for comedic effect). The bed has rumpled blankets. On the nightstand: a book titled *"Shanghan Lun"* lying open (he fell asleep reading it again), a cold cup of tea.

Above Ollie's head is a thought bubble showing a sequence of previous nights — all showing him waking up at roughly the same time (2:47 AM, 3:03 AM, 2:35 AM...) — suggesting this is a PATTERN, not random.

**Background Details:**
- Moonlight through window creates soft blue glow
- The bedroom should feel cozy but the wake-up frustration should be palpable
- Maybe a tiny Liver organ character (anthropomorphized liver, looking stressed/overworked) is visible through a semi-transparent "X-ray" effect in Ollie's midsection, waving a flag or holding a "We need to talk" sign

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "There's a time when you *always* feel off. You wake at 3 AM like clockwork. Your brain turns to mush at 2 PM. Appetite vanishes at noon. Coincidence? ...It is NOT."

**Mood:** The relatable "why does this keep happening?!" frustration mixed with curiosity.

---

### Panel 2: The Reveal — Your Organs Are Unionized

**Visual Description:**
This is the EXPLANATION panel — the big visual reveal. Show a circular CLOCK FACE (like a traditional Chinese timekeeping device or a modern 12-hour clock with TCM styling):

- The clock is divided into **12 segments** (each = 2 hours)
- Each segment has a cute/simple icon representing its organ:
  - 11PM-1AM: 💤 Gallbladder (sleeping icon)
  - 1-3AM: 🫁 Liver (cleansing/sparkle icon) ← highlighted/glowing!
  - 3-5AM: 🌬️ Lung (breath icon)
  - 5-7AM: 🚽 Large Intestine (subtle toilet humor OK here)
  - 7-9AM: 🔥 Stomach (fire/cooking icon)
  - 9-11AM: ⚡ Spleen (energy/battery icon)
  - 11AM-1PM: ❤️ Heart (brain/clarity icon)
  - 1-3PM: 🔍 Small Intestine (sorting/filter icon)
  - 3-5PM: 💧 Bladder (water droplet)
  - 5-7PM: 🌊 Kidney (deep water/essence icon)
  - 7-9PM: 🛡️ Pericardium (shield/protect icon)
  - 9-11PM: 🔗 Triple Burner (connection/coordination icon)

Ollie stands next to the clock, proudly presenting it like a tour guide, wearing a tiny conductor's hat or holding a pointer stick. The **Liver segment (1-3 AM)** is highlighted in RED/GLOWING to show "THIS IS YOUR PROBLEM."

**Background Details:**
- The clock can have a slightly ancient/modern fusion design — maybe a sundial-inspired outer ring with clean modern typography inside
- Small text labels for each time slot
- The overall impression should be "this is a real system, not made up"

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "Meet Ziwu Liuzhu — the TCM body clock. Twelve departments. Two-hour shifts. Your organs punch in and punch out on a schedule that predates mechanical clocks by about 1,800 years. And yours didn't ask permission."

**Mood:** Mind-blowing revelation meets organized satisfaction ("finally, an explanation!").

---

### Panel 3: The Three Patterns Everyone Knows

**Visual Description:**
Show three characters (could be simplified animal figures or stick figures with Ollie faces) experiencing the three most common patterns:

**Left — Pattern A (3 AM Wake-Up):**
A groggy figure sitting up in bed, alarm showing ~2:45 AM, thought bubble shows a frustrated liver character working overtime with a mop and bucket (cleaning/cleansing metaphor)

**Center — Pattern B (Afternoon Crash):**
A figure slumped over a desk around 2 PM, head on arms, a "Zzz" bubble coming from head, with a Small Intestine character nearby looking overwhelmed by a pile of "nutrients vs waste" sorting tasks

**Right — Pattern C (Morning No-Appetite):**
A figure at a breakfast table (7-9 AM) pushing away a plate of food, while a sad Stomach character sits nearby with nothing to do, checking its watch

Ollie stands in the center-bottom of the panel, gesturing to all three scenes with both wings spread, as if saying "SEE? You're not imagining this!"

**Ollie's Dialogue / Caption:**
> **Ollie:** "Three patterns everyone recognizes. 3 AM = Liver can't cleanse because yesterday's frustration is still queued up. 2 PM crash = Small Intestine buried under lunch. No morning appetite? Your Stomach showed up to work and found an empty inbox."

**Mood:** Recognition and validation — the reader should be nodding vigorously.

---

### Panel 4: Work With It — Track, Adjust, Wait

**Visual Description:**
Ollie is now in "action mode" — wearing a tiny detective hat or coach's whistle, holding a clipboard with a checklist. The mood is bright and proactive (morning sunlight, warm tones).

The checklist on Ollie's clipboard shows:
- ✅ Week 1: Track your rough spots (collect data, don't interpret)
- ✅ Match to the clock (does your pattern line up?)
- ✅ Make ONE adjustment tied to that window
- ⏳ Give it two weeks
- ❓ Still stuck? Get professional help

Around Ollie, show small visual hints of the adjustments:
- Someone walking outside for evening de-stress (Liver help)
- Someone having a lighter lunch (SI help)
- Someone eating dinner earlier (Stomach help)

End with Ollie looking satisfied, sipping from a fresh steaming mug of ginger tea, giving a thumbs-up (wing-up?).

**Ollie's Signature Line:**
> **Ollie:** "If your car's check engine light came on at the same time every day, you wouldn't call it coincidence. You'd check the engine log. This IS the engine log your body came with. Use it."

**Small signature tag at bottom-right corner:**
> *(Ollie's Liver files a noise complaint at 2:47 AM. He finally gets the message.)*

**Mood:** Empowering, practical, hopeful. The reader should feel equipped to take action tomorrow.

## General Production Notes

### Consistency Across All Comics:
1. **Ollie must look identical** in every panel and every comic — same proportions, same accessories (yin-yang necklace), same coloration
2. **Color palette locked** — caramel brown (#8B5E3C), sage green (#8BA888), cream white (#FDFAF5), accent variations allowed per mood
3. **Font hierarchy:**
   - Ollie dialogue: casual handwritten style (e.g., Caveat, Patrick Hand, or similar web font)
   - Labels/captions: clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Noto Sans)
   - Chinese terms: use italic serif for authenticity
4. **Panel borders:** thin rounded rectangles in accent color, or no border with subtle shadow separation
5. **Size target:** optimized for blog article width (~680px content area) while remaining crisp on retina displays

### Workflow Suggestion:
1. Sketch all 4 panels as thumbnails first (pencil or digital rough)
2. Refine composition and text placement
3. Final linework (ink/digital)
4. Flat color fill (no gradients unless minimal/atmospheric)
5. Text overlay (dialogue boxes or caption bars)
6. Export as WebP (for web optimization) + PNG (backup)
